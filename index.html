How Demha Works: Simple Dashboard Control for Local Businesses

Demha makes managing your online reputation simple, fast, and completely stress-free. After finishing a service appointment, simply log into your Demha dashboard and enter your customer's name and email address into the quick-send box. The system instantly dispatches a professional feedback request email to your client while their experience is still fresh. When they open the secure link, they select a star rating from 1 to 5. Delighted clients who leave 4 or 5 stars are instantly redirected to your public Google Business profile to boost your reviews. Meanwhile, any unhappy clients leaving 1 to 3 stars are intercepted privately and routed to an internal feedback form, alerting your management team right away to resolve the issue before it hits Google.

Enter customer details in your dashboard and let Demha handle the rest.
